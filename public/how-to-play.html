How to play Bonk Royale

Bonk Royale is a free multiplayer party game for kids and adults. You can learn it in about a minute.

1. Aim and flick

  • Phone or tablet: drag back anywhere on the screen, like a slingshot, then let go.
  • Mouse: point where you want to go. Click for a quick bonk, or hold the button to charge more power and let go.
  • Keyboard: turn with the left and right arrows (or A and D). Tap Space for a quick bonk, or hold it for more power.

2. Bonk your rivals

Crash into other creatures to shove them toward the edge. Faster flicks hit harder.

3. Stay on the island

Every few seconds the outer ring of tiles crumbles. Tiles that flash are about to fall, so head toward the middle.

4. Hearts and winning

You have three hearts. Fall off and you lose one, then come back with a shield. Lose all three and you are out. The last player standing wins.

5. Gems, orbs and pillars

Collect gems for coins. Glowing orbs give a shield, mega size, a rocket flick or a quake. Bounce off the striped pillars for tricky bank shots.

Play with friends

Share your friend code or an invite link, make a party and play a free-for-all, a 1 vs 1 duel or a team match. Choose Easy, Medium or Hard bots to fill the island.
