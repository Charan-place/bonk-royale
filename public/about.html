About Bonk Royale

Bonk Royale is a free party brawler that runs in your browser. There is nothing to download and nothing to sign up for. Pick a cute character, flick it across a candy island, and bonk your rivals off the edge. Last one standing wins.

How it plays

On a touch screen, drag back anywhere and let go to flick your character, like a slingshot. On a computer, point with the mouse and click (hold for more power), or use the arrow keys and Space. Hit other players to shove them toward the edge. Every few seconds the outer ring of the island crumbles, so the fight always comes to you. Each player has three hearts: fall off and you lose one and come back, lose all three and you are out.

Collect gems, grab power-up orbs (shield, mega size, rocket flick, quake), and use the pillars for bank shots.

Play with friends

Every player gets a friend code. Add a friend by their code, or tap Add Friend on someone you just played against. Invite friends into a party, pick a mode (brawl, 1 vs 1 duel, or teams), and play together. There is no chat. Players may choose a short display name (letters and numbers only, checked by a filter), so playing stays friendly and safe for everyone.

Fair play

All gameplay is decided by the server. Rewards, capsules and unlocks are earned by playing, and nothing you can buy makes you stronger.
