Bonk Royale: free online multiplayer party game

Flick your cute creature, bonk your friends off a crumbling candy island and be the last one standing. Free to play in your browser, no download, for kids and adults.

Questions

Is Bonk Royale free?

Yes. Bonk Royale is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

How do you play Bonk Royale?

Aim and flick your creature to crash into rivals and knock them off a crumbling candy island. Stay on the tiles as the edge falls away and be the last one standing.

Can I play with friends?

Yes. Share a friend code or a party link to play together, or play against bots to practise. You can choose Free for all, 1 vs 1 or Teams, and Easy, Medium or Hard bots.

Is Bonk Royale safe for kids?

There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.

Are there other Bonk games?

Yes. Bonk Games also has Bonk Golf, Bonk Merge, Hot Potato Bonk, Bonk Rush and a Daily puzzle, all free and all using the same account and level.
