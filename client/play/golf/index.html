Bonk Golf: free online game

Bonk Golf is a free flick-and-aim mini golf game in your browser. Pull back, let go and send your cute creature into the hole through bumpers, slippery ice and portals.

How to play

Drag back from your creature and release to flick it toward the hole. On a keyboard use the arrow keys to aim and hold Space for power. Finish a course in fewer shots to earn up to 3 stars. There are 40 courses.

Who is it for?

Easy enough for kids around 5 and more, with tricky courses that adults enjoy. A round takes about a minute.

Questions

Is Bonk Golf free?

Yes. Bonk Golf is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

How many courses does Bonk Golf have?

Bonk Golf has 40 courses, each worth up to 3 stars, and your best results are saved to your profile.

How do I play Bonk Golf on a phone?

Drag back from the creature with your finger and let go to flick it. Fewer shots earn more stars.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest without an account, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.
