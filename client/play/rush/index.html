Bonk Rush: free online game

Bonk Rush is a free one-thumb endless runner. Your creature runs across icy platforms on its own. Tap to jump, grab gems, dodge cones and see how far you can go.

How to play

Tap to jump and tap again in the air to jump twice. On a computer click, press Space or press the Up arrow.

Who is it for?

Instant to learn for kids, hard to put down for adults. A run takes under a minute.

Questions

Is Bonk Rush free?

Yes. Bonk Rush is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

What are the controls in Bonk Rush?

Just one: tap, click or press Space to jump. Tap again in the air for a double jump.

Is there a leaderboard in Bonk Rush?

Yes. Your best distance is saved and ranked on the leaderboard.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest without an account, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.
