Bonk Merge: free online game

Bonk Merge is a free relaxing merge puzzle. Drop creatures into the box, and when two of the same kind touch they merge into a bigger one. Aim for the Legendary Bonk.

How to play

Slide to choose where to drop, then lift your finger to let go. On a computer move the mouse and click, or use the arrow keys and Space. Do not let the pile reach the top line.

Who is it for?

A calm game for all ages. Good for quick breaks.

Questions

Is Bonk Merge free?

Yes. Bonk Merge is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

How do you win Bonk Merge?

There is no fixed ending. Keep merging creatures into bigger ones to reach the Legendary Bonk and beat your own best score.

What ends a game of Bonk Merge?

The game ends when the pile of creatures stays above the top line, so leave room and plan your merges.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest without an account, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.
