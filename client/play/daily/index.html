Daily Bonk: free online game

Daily Bonk is a free daily puzzle. Crack the secret lineup of 4 creatures in 8 tries. Everyone gets the same puzzle each day, and a new one arrives every day.

How to play

Pick four creatures (they can repeat) and press Guess. A green dot means the right creature in the right spot. A yellow dot means the right creature in the wrong spot. On a computer use keys 1 to 6 and Enter.

Who is it for?

A short brain game for all ages. Come back every day to keep your streak alive.

Questions

Is Daily Bonk free?

Yes. Daily Bonk is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

How often does Daily Bonk change?

A new puzzle arrives every day, and it is the same puzzle for every player.

How many guesses do I get in Daily Bonk?

You get 8 guesses to find the secret lineup of 4 creatures.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest without an account, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.
