Hot Potato Bonk: free online game

Hot Potato Bonk is a free party game in your browser. A bomb jumps between creatures when they bump, and whoever holds it when the fuse ends is out. Be the last one standing.

How to play

Hold to steer your creature and tap to dash into a rival and pass the bomb on. On a computer use the mouse or WASD to steer and Space to dash.

Who is it for?

Quick rounds that suit kids and adults alike. Rivals are bots, so you can play alone any time.

Questions

Is Hot Potato Bonk free?

Yes. Hot Potato Bonk is free to play in your browser on phones, tablets and computers. There is nothing to download or install.

Who do I play against in Hot Potato Bonk?

You play against computer-controlled bots, so you can start a round straight away without waiting for other players.

How do I pass the bomb?

Bump into another creature. The bomb moves to them, so time your dash and keep away from the others.

Is it safe for kids?

Yes. There is no chat, and players only see a made-up game name. You can play as a guest without an account, and signing in with Google is optional and for ages 13 and over.
